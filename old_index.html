Project Domain

This section has domain details about our project. Here we describe the information we have gathered by the literature survey, our objectives, and the methodology.

Literature Survey

Existing research has explored several AI-based approaches for improving digital service platforms:

  • AI-based Service Recognition – identifies and interprets service requirements from user inputs.
  • Provider Recommendation – helps identify suitable service providers based on user requirements.
  • Predictive Scheduling – considers provider availability and service constraints for better scheduling.
  • Demand Forecasting – predicts future service demand using historical and contextual data.
  • Generative AI – supports automated creation of service-related promotional content.

Research observation: Many existing approaches focus on individual functions rather than addressing the complete local service marketplace lifecycle.

Research Gap

Despite the growing use of AI in digital service platforms, several gaps remain:

  • Fragmented AI Solutions – Existing studies often address service recognition, provider recommendation, scheduling, or demand forecasting separately.
  • Limited Provider Evidence – Provider capabilities are commonly based on self-declared skills rather than completed-work evidence.
  • Limited Context Integration – Information generated at one stage is not consistently carried through the entire service lifecycle.
  • Limited Support for Local Providers – Small-scale service providers often lack integrated tools for service representation and automated promotion.
  • Uncertainty Handling – Many systems require stronger mechanisms for handling uncertain AI predictions before using them in marketplace decisions.

Research Need: A unified approach is required to connect these capabilities while maintaining reliable and context-aware information throughout the local service marketplace lifecycle.

Research Problem

Local service providers often struggle to build a professional digital presence and reach suitable customers, while customers face difficulties finding reliable and capable providers. Existing solutions generally address individual aspects such as service discovery, matching, or booking rather than providing an integrated AI-driven solution.

Therefore, there is a need for an AI-driven platform that connects customers and local service providers while supporting intelligent service discovery, provider matching, portfolio management, and proactive recommendations.

Research Objectives

  • Develop an AI-driven local service platform – Connect customers and local service providers through an intelligent digital platform.
  • Improve service discovery and provider matching – Use AI to understand service requirements and recommend suitable providers based on relevant factors.
  • Support providers with AI-powered tools – Enable automated portfolio creation, service categorization, and professional advertisement generation.
  • Enable proactive service demand management – Use demand forecasting to identify future service needs and provide timely notifications to customers and providers.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed system – Assess the system’s accuracy, usability, performance, and user satisfaction through experiments and user feedback.

Methodology

The proposed system follows a design-and-development methodology to integrate AI techniques into a unified local service marketplace. The methodology includes data collection, AI model development, system integration, and evaluation.

Main Steps:

  • Data Collection & Preprocessing – Collect and prepare service-related images and marketplace data.
  • AI Model Development – Apply image classification, service recognition, provider recommendation, and demand forecasting techniques.
  • System Development – Integrate the AI models with the customer, provider, and marketplace modules.
  • AI-Assisted Services – Generate provider advertisements, recommend suitable providers, and provide proactive demand notifications.
  • Evaluation – Evaluate model performance, system functionality, usability, and user feedback.

This follows the common approach of combining AI-based service understanding, matching, and marketplace functions into an integrated workflow.
